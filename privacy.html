Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Social Tracker ("the app") is a private, internal analytics tool used by our team to monitor the performance of social media accounts that we own or manage. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

Only after the owner of an account logs in and explicitly authorizes the app, we read, through the official TikTok and Instagram APIs:

  • basic profile information (display name, username, avatar);
  • account statistics (followers, following, total likes, number of videos);
  • the list of public videos and posts with their statistics (views, likes, comments, shares, captions, publication date, links and cover images).

The app is read-only: it never publishes, edits or deletes content, and it does not access private messages.

How we use the data

The data is used only to show the accounts' performance in a password-protected dashboard reserved to our team and to estimate the earnings of our own content partnerships. We do not sell, rent or share the data with third parties and we do not use it for advertising.

Storage and security

Data is stored in a private repository. Access tokens are stored encrypted. The published dashboard data is encrypted and can be opened only with a password known to our team.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the account remains connected. The account owner can revoke access at any time from the TikTok or Instagram app settings; after that no new data is collected. To request the deletion of the stored data, contact us at sightsevenn@gmail.com.

Contact

For any question about this policy: sightsevenn@gmail.com.

Informativa sulla privacy
