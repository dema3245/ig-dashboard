1. The service

Social Tracker ("the app") is a private, internal analytics tool that our team uses to view statistics of social media accounts we own or manage. It is not a public service and it is not sold or offered to third parties.

2. Who can use it

Only members of our team and the owners of the connected accounts may use the app. Connecting an account requires the account owner to log in and authorize read-only access through the official TikTok or Instagram login.

3. What the app does

The app reads profile information, account statistics and statistics of public videos and posts, and shows them in a password-protected dashboard. It never publishes, edits or deletes content on behalf of the user.

4. Revoking access

The account owner can revoke the app's access at any time from the TikTok or Instagram settings. Data handling is described in our Privacy Policy.

5. Third-party platforms

Use of TikTok and Instagram remains subject to their own terms. The app uses only their official APIs and complies with their developer policies.

6. No warranty

The statistics and estimates shown are provided "as is" for internal use and may differ from the figures shown by the platforms.
